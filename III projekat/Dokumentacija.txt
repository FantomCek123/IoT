PODIZANJE INFRASTRUKTURE I KREIRANJE CEP STRIMA (1 TERMINAL)
    1.
    docker compose up -d

    2. Kreiranje eKuiper strima:
    $streamBody = '{"sql": "CREATE STREAM iot_stream () WITH (DATASOURCE=\"iot_sensor_data\", FORMAT=\"json\", TYPE=\"mqtt\", CONF_KEY=\"default\")"}'
    Invoke-RestMethod -Uri "http://localhost:9081/streams" -Method Post -Body $streamBody -ContentType "application/json"

    3. Kreiranje eKuiper pravila:
    $ruleBody = '{"id": "rule_ekuiper_alarm", "sql": "SELECT temperature, \"ALARM: Visoka temperatura!\" as poruka FROM iot_stream WHERE temperature > 50", "actions": [{"mqtt": {"server": "tcp://iot_mosquitto_p2:1883", "topic": "sensors/ekuiper_alarms", "sendSingle": true}}]}'
    Invoke-RestMethod -Uri "http://localhost:9081/rules" -Method Post -Body $ruleBody -ContentType "application/json"


PROVERA RADNOG STANJA MAAS SERVIS ENDPOINT-A (1 TERMINAL)
    Slanje testne normalne temperature na ML model:
    Invoke-RestMethod -Uri "http://localhost:8050/predict" -Method Post -Body '{"temperature": 24.5}' -ContentType "application/json"

    Slanje testne kritične temperature na ML model:
    Invoke-RestMethod -Uri "http://localhost:8050/predict" -Method Post -Body '{"temperature": 65.5}' -ContentType "application/json"
